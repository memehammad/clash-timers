Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

Clash Timers collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your village export

The export you copy from Clash of Clans is read and stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only to import a village export. With optional Shizuku auto-import, it checks the clipboard only while Clash of Clans is open and ignores anything that isn't an export.

Internet

The only network request is a check of GitHub's public releases page to tell you about app updates. GitHub may see your IP address, as with any website visit.

Notifications and alarms

Used only to tell you when timers finish. Everything is scheduled on the device.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
