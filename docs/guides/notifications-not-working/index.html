Clash of Clans notifications not working?

Upgrade done, builder idle, and your phone stayed quiet? You're not alone. Here's why the game's notifications go missing on Android, what to check, and a way to get alerts that always arrive.

Why the alerts go missing

Clash of Clans sends its notifications from Supercell's servers through Google's push service. Several things on the phone can stop them along the way: notifications turned off for the game, Android putting the app to sleep to save battery, Do Not Disturb, or a data saver that blocks the game in the background. If you play on more than one device, the alert may also land on the other one.

Fix it step by step

  1. Allow notifications. Long-press the Clash of Clans icon, tap App info, then Notifications, and make sure they're on, including every category below the main switch.
  2. Check the game's own settings. In Clash of Clans, open Settings and make sure notifications are switched on there too.
  3. Stop battery optimisation. In App info, open Battery and choose Unrestricted (or "Don't optimise"). Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus and Huawei phones also have their own sleeping-apps lists. Take the game off them.
  4. Allow background data. In App info, open Mobile data and turn on background data and unrestricted data usage.
  5. Check Do Not Disturb and modes. Make sure Clash of Clans isn't silenced by Do Not Disturb, Bedtime, or a focus mode.
  6. Open the game once on this phone. If you also play on a tablet or another phone, the last device you played on is the safest bet for alerts.
  7. Update and restart. Update the game from the Play Store, then restart your phone.

Alerts that always arrive

Even with everything set up, server push notifications can arrive late or not at all. Clash Timers works differently: it reads the game's Data Export and schedules the alerts on your phone, with Android's alarm system. No server and no internet are involved, so they arrive on time, even in flight mode.

  • A notification for every finished upgrade, research and pet, including the Goblin Builder.
  • Active Builder and Research Potions are counted, so alerts move earlier when you boost.
  • An optional reminder when a builder sits idle for 30 minutes or more.
  • A home screen widget that shows free builders at a glance.
