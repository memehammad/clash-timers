Clash of Clans notifications not working?

Upgrade done, builder idle, and your phone stayed quiet? Clash Timers fixes that: alerts that come from your own phone, on time, every time.

Why the game's alerts go missing

Clash of Clans sends its notifications from Supercell's servers over the internet. On the way, they can be delayed or dropped: by battery saving, notification settings, a weak connection, or because you last played on another device.

Clash Timers: alerts from your own phone

Clash Timers reads the game's Data Export and schedules every alert on your phone, with Android's alarm system. No server and no internet are involved, so the alerts arrive on time, even in flight mode.

  • Every finish: upgrades, research, pets, Builder Base and the Goblin Builder.
  • Boosts counted: Builder and Research Potions move the alert earlier.
  • Idle reminder: a nudge if a builder sits free for 30 minutes or more.
  • A home screen widget showing free builders at a glance.
  • Free, open source, no login.
  1. Install Clash Timers and allow notifications in the quick setup.
  2. In Clash of Clans, copy your village: Settings, More Settings, Data Export, Copy.
  3. Open Clash Timers. Every running upgrade now has its alert.

Still want to fix the game's own notifications?

Long-press the Clash of Clans icon, tap App info and check that notifications are on, battery is set to Unrestricted and background data is allowed. Also check Do Not Disturb, and open the game once on the phone you want alerts on.
