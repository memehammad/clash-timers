How to use the Clash of Clans Data Export

Clash of Clans can copy your whole village as text. Here's where to find it, what's inside and how to put it to use.

Where to find the Data Export

  1. Open Clash of Clans and tap Settings (the gear icon).
  2. Tap More Settings.
  3. Scroll to Data Export and tap Copy. Your village is now on the clipboard.

What's in the export

The export is a block of text (JSON) with your player tag and a snapshot of your village: buildings, traps, heroes, troops, spells and pets with their levels, plus every upgrade that's running right now and how much time it has left. Builder Base, the Builder's Apprentice, Lab Assistant and active boosts are in there too.

It doesn't include your Supercell ID, password or anything that lets someone log in, so it's safe to paste into tools you trust.

What you can do with it

Because the export lists every running timer, an app can work out when each builder, the laboratory and the pet house will be free. Clash Timers does exactly that on Android: paste the export once and you get a home screen widget, a notification when each upgrade finishes, and a planner that shows what a Builder Potion would finish.

Keeping it up to date

The export is a snapshot. Copy a new one after you start upgrades or use a potion. With the optional Shizuku auto-import, Clash Timers picks up each new export on its own as soon as you tap Copy.
